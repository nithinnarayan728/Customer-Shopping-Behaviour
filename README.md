# Customer-Shopping-Behaviour

1.	Project Overview
This project studies customer shopping habits using data from 3,900 purchases across different product categories. The main goal is to understand how much customers spend, what products they prefer, how often they shop, and whether they use subscription or discounts. These insights can help businesses understand their customers better and make better business decisions.

2 . Dataset Summary
- Rows – 3,900
- Columns – 18
- 
  Key Features
-	Customer demographics : Age, Gender, Location and Subscription Status
-	Purchase Details – Item purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Size, Season , Colour.
-	Shopping Behaviour – Discount Applied, Promocode Applied, Frequency of Purchase, Previous Purchases, Review and Shipping details.

Source - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/iamsouravbanerjee/customer-shopping-trends-dataset?resource
